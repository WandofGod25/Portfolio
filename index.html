About me

I am a product and design leader building digital experiences from 0-1. Over the years, I've led design initiatives across multiple domains, building end-to-end user experiences and brand identities that resonate.

In my work, I'm always oriented towards deeply understanding the users' needs and pain points. Great design should always check 3 main traits: clarity, usability, and aesthetics.

I thrive at the intersection of strategy, design, and technology. I value a high ownership - high flexibility approach, sitting at the table where the important decisions determine the product's future vision.

Outside of work, you'll find me prototyping new ideas, testing AI tools or making music.
